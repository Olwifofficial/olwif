# Network logos

Locally served network logo assets from [Web3 Icons](https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons), commit `f6881ce9606c86103cceac250b01df3f8867620d`.

Original directory: https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons/tree/f6881ce9606c86103cceac250b01df3f8867620d/packages/core/src/svgs/networks/background

Fetched 2026-09-24. SVG artwork is unchanged; binance-smart-chain.svg is saved as bsc.svg, and arbitrum-one.svg as arbitrum.svg to match OLWIF network identifiers.

MIT licence is included in LICENCE.txt. Network names and logos remain the marks of their respective owners. Display indicates selectable network coverage, not endorsement or a guarantee that every token has available data.

The visible list is derived from NETWORKS in lib/security.ts. Auto-detect is not a network and does not get a logo. Assets are served locally: no icon CDN, client-side fetch loop or additional runtime dependency.

Robinhood Chain is additionally supported and appears as a plain-text link to its official documentation. Do not substitute a generic Robinhood brokerage logo for the chain mark. Its mainnet configuration (4663, robinhoodchain.blockscout.com) was verified against https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/deploy-smart-contracts/ on 2026-09-24. Network support does not mean all data providers cover the chain.

## Trademark review before public launch

The icon library's MIT licence is not evidence that the library can grant trademark rights in the networks' branding. No individual trademark permission or endorsement agreement has been verified. The non-affiliation notice at `/terms#third-party-marks` explains the display purpose; it is not permission or a legal clearance.

Before public launch, confirm the intended use against each rights holder's current brand policy, use their approved artwork as required, obtain permission if needed, and add the operator's public rights-holder contact details. Have the terms and actual presentation reviewed for the operator's jurisdiction. Replace uncertain logo uses with text links if clearance cannot be established.

Initial primary guidance checked 2026-09-24 (not a complete clearance review):

- https://solana.com/branding
- https://brand.base.org/core-identifiers
- https://www.bnbchain.org/en/brand-guidelines
- https://arbitrum.io/brand-kit
- https://polygon.technology/brand-guidelines
- https://optimism.io/brand
- https://www.gov.uk/using-somebody-elses-intellectual-property/trade-marks

Logo links open the official websites in a new tab with `noopener noreferrer`, without tracking parameters. Blast's homepage currently promotes its broader mobile/yield product; its chain-specific information is at https://docs.blast.io/about-blast. A link is not a recommendation to transact.
